\section{Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations}
\paperhead{Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations}{Tianyu Gao, Howard Yen, Jiatong Yu, Danqi Chen}{EMNLP 2023}{https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627}{arXiv API id\_list=2305.14627, title match 1.00}

\subsection{What the paper does}

Citations are the standard remedy for hallucination in information seeking systems, and products such as Bing Chat ship them. But the remedy is unmeasured: prior work evaluates attribution by hand against closed commercial search engines, so no two systems can be compared and no modelling choice credited with a gain.

ALCE fixes the questions and the corpus and requires an end to end system to retrieve, generate and cite, scoring fluency, correctness and citation quality automatically. The load bearing move: citation quality is decided by an entailment model asking whether the cited passages support the sentence, not whether the citation dereferences.

\subsection{How the method works}

Given a query and corpus $\mathcal{D}$, a system returns statements $s_1 \dots s_n$, each with a citation set $\mathcal{C}_i \subseteq \mathcal{D}$, split at sentence boundaries, at most three citations each, over 100 word passages. ASQA (ambiguous factoid) and QAMPARI (list valued) use a 21M passage Wikipedia snapshot, ELI5 uses Sphere, a filtered Common Crawl of 899M passages; 1,000 development examples each, no training data.

Fluency is MAUVE; correctness is exact match recall on ASQA, precision and recall-5 on QAMPARI, and claim recall on ELI5, where text-davinci-003 decomposes gold answers into sub-claims an NLI model checks. Citation quality reuses that model, TRUE, a T5-11B checkpoint fine tuned on an NLI mixture. Citation recall for $s_i$ is 1 exactly when $\mathcal{C}_i$ is non-empty and $\phi(\mathrm{concat}(\mathcal{C}_i), s_i) = 1$; precision is a leave one out test, where $c$ is ``irrelevant'' iff $\phi(c, s_i) = 0$ and $\phi(\mathrm{concat}(\mathcal{C}_i \setminus \{c\}), s_i) = 1$, credited only where recall is already 1. Retrievers are GTR, DPR and BM25; prompting spans VANILLA to CLOSEDBOOK, with RERANK and POSTCITE as post-edits.

\subsection{What it reports}

On ASQA with ChatGPT VANILLA, citation recall is 73.6 and precision 72.5; RERANK raises them to 84.8 and 81.6 while correctness stays flat. ELI5 is far harder at 51.1 and 50.0, and around 50\% of ChatGPT and GPT-4 generations are not fully supported by the passages they cite. Post-hoc citing fails: CLOSEDBOOK beats VANILLA on correctness for ELI5 and QAMPARI, yet POSTCITE leaves citation recall 47\% below VANILLA on ASQA. Cohen's kappa against annotators is 0.698 for citation recall.

\subsection{How Mytheus uses this}
\begin{takeaway}
The golden-set harness asserts citations resolve 100 percent, a plumbing test: it proves \texttt{meeting\_id} and \texttt{chunk\_id} dereference, not that the passage supports the sentence. Replace it with ALCE citation recall and precision over the RAG \texttt{cite} output: split answers into sentences, concatenate each sentence's bound passages, and score entailment with a cross-encoder NLI head on Ray Serve. Report both per asker role, since \texttt{expand} personalises variants via \texttt{get\_user\_context} and ALCE defines no such split, which is the delta Mytheus can claim.

None of it runs online: TRUE is an 11B pass per statement and RERANK needs four generations, far outside the 500 ms warm RAG p95 or ContextGapAgent's 1.5 s round trip. Keep it in CI and the canary; the live contract stays the drop-uncited rule plus the 0.7 gate. ALCE validity is purely semantic too, so a passage entailing the sentence from another tenant's collection still scores 1: conjoin it with an \texttt{org\_id} check.
\end{takeaway}

\subsection{Limitations and what it leaves open}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt\parskip0pt
  \item Every corpus is a static offline snapshot, never streaming ASR: diarised speaker-attributed claims, mutable interim transcripts and live-window citations go untested.
  \item One answer per question against one gold reference, with no asker role, so an answer grounded in evidence irrelevant to that reader scores perfectly.
  \item No configuration is measured for latency or cost, so serveability is untested.
  \item Abstention is never rewarded: an empty citation set simply scores recall 0.
  \item The leave one out rule cannot express partial support, and multi-hop, mathematical and code questions are excluded.
\end{itemize}
